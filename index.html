The Effect of Autocorrect on Typing Speed and Accuracy

Prototype for instructor review only. This file stores data only in the browser where it is used. It does not send participant results to you from other phones. Do not collect real participant data until your instructor approves the procedure, consent, and data storage.

Welcome

This study examines whether autocorrect affects typing speed and the number of errors remaining in a completed passage. You will complete two short typing tasks: one with autocorrect ON and one with autocorrect OFF. Participation is voluntary.

The checkbox is a prototype control, not an approved electronic consent process.

Preparation

Prepare your keyboard

iPhone instructions

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select General, then Keyboard.
  3. Find Auto-Correction and switch it ON or OFF as directed above.
  4. Return to this browser tab.

Predictive Text is a separate setting. Change only Auto-Correction unless your instructor approves otherwise.

Android instructions

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Find System or General management, then Keyboard / On-screen keyboard.
  3. Choose your keyboard (such as Gboard or Samsung Keyboard).
  4. Find Text correction and change Auto-correction as directed.
  5. Return to this browser tab.

Menu names vary by device and keyboard.

Use your device's keyboard settings to change autocorrect as directed. Ask the researcher if you cannot find the setting.

Important: The website cannot change or verify your phone's autocorrect setting. Please follow the instructions and confirm the setting yourself.

Task 1 of 2
